IMPLEMENTATION PRINCIPLES
An action should have anticipation, one clear peak, a readable hold where appropriate, follow-through and a controlled return. Weight comes from stance, joints, acceleration and contact, never a rubber torso. Ordinary blocky pet geometry stays rigid; uniform whole-model shrinking is reserved for lifecycle disappearance. Do not add weapons, attacks, damage, fighting or combat merely because a pose is called a power move.

Use one state/timeline owner per active throw: idle -> charge -> submit -> flight -> land -> gate -> entrance -> showcase -> capture -> card -> cleanup. The server controls validation, distance, outcome, pending state and reward. The client owns presentation, never awards inventory or Strength from a tween completion. Events may arrive late, duplicate or out of order: key everything by throwId, reconcile to server state, and have bounded fallbacks.

CHARGE
Retain the current 1.5-second ping-pong cycle, perfect threshold power >=0.92 and perfect-distance multiplier 1.15. Show the correct power/distance/zone/luck using shared formulas and valid lane clamps. Set feet/support first, wind the throwing shoulder back, rotate torso slightly, keep the held orb aligned to the hand and build floor/orb energy with caps. Character pose begins blending in over about 0.12–0.18s. Avoid continuously rebuilding the orb. The avatar must not turn away from the authoritative +Z throw lane because the camera faces elsewhere.

RELEASE / FLIGHT
Release pose: about 0.12–0.2s forward arm motion, then 0.2–0.35s recovery. Held orb disappears only into a coherent confirmed projectile presentation; a rejection resets pose/input/sound without playing a false success. Keep R6/R15 joint differences and text/UI focus handling correct.
Server flight duration remains clamp(0.7+sqrt(distance)*0.07,0.9,4.5). Endpoints remain authoritative. Use a tangent-aligned quadratic arc, restrained spin and one tapered trail. Camera follows only the owner's throw, with smoothing and collision-aware framing. Short throws need less camera movement. Do not apply someone else's camera shake/zoom globally.

LAND / OPEN
Begin ground contact at the server flight endpoint. First bounce about 0.18–0.22s, second about 0.12–0.14s and much smaller; then a short energy settle. Gate unfolds in about 0.4–0.5s, coordinated with the current server PortalOpened schedule (flight+0.9s). Avoid three independent timers that cause the creature to spawn before the gate is open. Orb/gate theme comes from the equipped portal, while species pool comes from landing biome; do not confuse those systems.

Use an actual readable ring frame, contained inner energy and a small local shockwave. No full-screen strobe or blanket particle cloud. Ground dust/lava/snow accents must fit the reached biome and stay clear of the creature face. Root, gate center and exit direction must be consistent with the real ground plane.
