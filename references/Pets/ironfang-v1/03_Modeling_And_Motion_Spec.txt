IRONFANG — VISUAL APPROVAL AND BUILD BRIEF
Status: proposed art direction, awaiting the owner's visual approval.
These are modeling/pose references, not completed models or animation clips.
The live roster and shared Studio place have not been changed by this study.

REFERENCE ORDER
1. 01_Rarity_Family.png defines the anatomy, materials and rarity progression.
2. 02_Motion_Keyposes.png defines the pose language.
3. This document defines motion timing, contacts and implementation constraints.
The corrected Depart panel faces RIGHT into the portal; Return faces LEFT out.
The pose sheet is not a sprite atlas or a complete sequence of animation frames.

PURPOSE
Calibrate one wolf family before extending the visual language across the roster.
Retain distinct species identities when translating the style to rabbits, birds,
stags, golems and dragons. Ironfang is a proposed study family, not a rename of
every existing creature. Game code uses "Mythical"; the art sheet says "Mythic".

ANATOMY THAT STAYS CONSISTENT
- Stern jade slit eyes, squared projecting muzzle, pointed ears and cheek planes.
- Wedge chest, cuboid ribcage, narrower waist, defined neck and solid haunches.
- Four articulated legs, large beveled paws, two toe seams, segmented tail.
- Rigid faceted surfaces. Chest, belly and muzzle do not inflate or squash.
- Clear joints and space between limbs; armor follows the supporting body part.
- Readable silhouette at follow-camera distance and in the pet picker.

RARITY PROGRESSION
Common / Scout: clean stone/steel planes, modest brass collar, eyes as the only
light accent. Simple construction and restrained detail, with a strong silhouette.
Epic / Sentinel: forged steel, layered shoulders/shins, three dorsal plates,
small jade inlays, gold fasteners and segmented tail protection.
Legendary / Sovereign: pale ivory/silver, refined gold edges, three-prong crest,
sculpted chest gem and deliberate layered armor. Face/ears remain unobstructed.
Mythical / Ascendant: pinnacle of this sample family; five-prong crest, jade core,
two rigid swept-back crystal fins, layered foreleg armor and a blade-like tail.
At most three small floating gold fragments behind the shoulders.
Do not derive rarity differences only from recoloring or uniform scaling.

MATERIAL STARTING POINTS
Pale stone/silver: #CBC8C0; dark forged steel: #5E6267; gold: #C6A05B;
jade inlay: #3A9D77; bright ivory: #F3E9D1; deeper trim: #3D4948.
These are authoring swatches. Judge them in the real game lighting.
Keep emissive areas small; the geometry must remain legible with all VFX disabled.

SCALE AND RIG
Treat the pictures as silhouette references, not engineering measurements.
Use the existing Config rarity scale once; do not multiply rarity size twice.
Fit the body, crest, fins and tail against current pad spacing and follow gaps.
Draft Common dimensions to refine: torso about 2.6 studs long, chest 1.35 wide,
standing shoulder height about 1.8, paws about 0.46 wide by 0.60 long.
Adjust these after comparing the silhouette and actual slot spacing.

Use the existing CreatureBuilder/SpeciesProfiles/CreatureAnimator rig contract.
A suitable conceptual hierarchy is root > body > neck > head > jaw/ears;
body/pelvis > upper leg > lower leg > paw for FL/FR/RL/RR; body > tail segments.
Map this to actual existing rig fields rather than introducing incompatible names.
Armor is rigidly attached to the body segment it protects. The display rig uses
a stable root and controlled joints; it must not depend on ragdoll simulation.
Model root placement and visual bounds must work in both world and ViewportFrames.

MOTION RULES
Drive locomotion phase from distance traveled to avoid foot sliding. Cycle times
below are authoring references at a nominal speed, not changes to follow speed.
A planted paw stays fixed relative to the ground during its stance interval.
Absorb weight through joint angles; never scale the torso into a soft shape.
All angles below are starting limits to refine in the isolated preview.

IDLE — 3.2 second loop
0.00: four paws planted, neutral head, tail still.
0.00-1.60: tiny neck/chest lift through joints (under 1% of body height).
1.60-3.20: return to neutral. No torso scaling.
One slow head scan up to 5 degrees; occasional 3-degree ear adjustment.
Common is alert; Epic is watchful; Legendary is proud; Mythical is composed.

WALK — nominal 0.80 second cycle
Four-beat sequence: left hind, left fore, right hind, right fore, spaced through
the cycle. Each paw spends roughly 65% in stance and 35% in swing.
Shoulder/hip swing starts near +/-20 degrees; knee lift clears the ground.
Torso pitch stays within 2 degrees; tail counters heading by about 5 degrees.
Scale stride with body size and synchronize it to actual traveled distance.

RUN — nominal 0.50 second gallop cycle
0.00-0.12: hind-leg drive; neck extends by joint angle.
0.12-0.22: short suspension; forelegs reach forward, body shape remains fixed.
0.22-0.36: forepaw contacts and elbows absorb load; hind legs recover underneath.
0.36-0.50: hind paws plant for the next drive.
Keep suspension short. Torso pitch within about 3 degrees. No stretching.

TURN — 0.35 second blend for a 45-90 degree heading adjustment
0.00-0.08: head leads by up to 15 degrees.
0.08-0.28: body follows, inside stride shortens, outside stride widens.
0.28-0.35: settle head/body alignment; tail counters by up to 20 degrees.
Do not rotate the whole planted model like a sliding toy.

STOP — 0.22 second deceleration plus 0.18 second settle
Shorten the final stride and plant forepaws before collecting the hind stance.
Lower the body slightly by bending joints, then recover the proud neutral pose.
Do not bounce, deform paws, or continue stepping after root velocity stops.

LEAP AND LAND — about 0.70 second action, adjusted to the actual path
0.00-0.12: anticipation through hind-knee bend; body shape unchanged.
0.12-0.20: push-off with hind-leg extension.
0.20-0.43: airborne arc; forelegs tuck, tail stabilizes.
0.43-0.55: forelegs prepare to reach the ground.
0.55-0.62: front contact, elbows bend.
0.62-0.70: hind contact and settle.
Use actual ground height. Front contact precedes hind contact; no floor penetration.

PORTAL CAPTURE — about 0.70 second visual reaction
0.00-0.18: head turns toward the portal.
0.18-0.50: controlled root lift/pull with forelegs collected.
0.50-0.70: short whole-model fade into the portal.
The visual must follow the existing authoritative capture result; it does not
create or award a creature independently. Never stretch the wolf into a ribbon.

EXPEDITION DEPART — about 1.30 seconds
0.00-0.35: two purposeful approach steps.
0.35-0.55: brief glance toward the player, then refocus on the portal.
0.55-0.95: leap INTO the portal, head and forepaws first.
0.95-1.10: body and hind legs cross the portal plane.
1.10-1.30: tail passes and the visual fades.
The accepted server trip owns availability and timing. If needed, use a temporary
local visual clone for the departure after the authoritative pet leaves the base.

EXPEDITION RETURN — about 1.60 seconds
0.00-0.25: portal cue, restrained light.
0.25-0.65: head/forelegs emerge, then body and hindquarters.
0.65-0.85: front/hind landing contacts.
0.85-1.15: stabilize and look toward the player.
1.15-1.60: confident proud stance, then transition to idle.
Existing server claim logic awards the payout once. This animation is presentation.

VFX AND ACCESSIBILITY
Common: no particle aura. Epic: small inlays. Legendary: restrained crest/chest
glow. Mythical: small controlled fragments and crystal accents.
The character must look complete with VFX disabled. Honor Reduced Motion,
Reduced Flashes and Low VFX; use a short fade/settle instead of a cinematic leap
when motion is reduced. Do not add floating notification boxes.

BUILD SEQUENCE AFTER VISUAL APPROVAL

2. Build the Common neutral silhouette in an isolated preview area.

4. Lock head/body/paws/tail before adding the other three armor tiers.
5. Build each rarity with separate rigid attachments and shared joint hierarchy.
6. Add idle, walk and run; verify paw contacts before portal actions.
7. Add turn, stop, leap, land, capture, depart and return.
8. Inspect all tiers in world, pet picker, follow formation and a full courtyard.
9. Check reduced-motion/low-VFX and current performance budgets.
10. Integrate through existing IDs/rig fields only after the preview matches.
Keep existing odds, income, saves, ownership, Treasury and mission rules intact.
