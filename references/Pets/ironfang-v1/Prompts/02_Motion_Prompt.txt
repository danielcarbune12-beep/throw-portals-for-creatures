NO crown, no crystal wings, no Mythic floating fragments. Four-legged animal, not humanoid.
Create one high-resolution white studio animation pose sheet, two rows of four equally sized panels, eight full-body key poses. Title "IRONFANG SENTINEL". Subtitle "Rigid-body motion study". Each panel has just one exact short label and one readable animal pose; no long text, no frame borders, faint baseline and light ground-contact shadow. Retain the SAME anatomy, materials and proportions in all poses. Crisp blocky low-poly hard geometry, articulated joints, no soft bending or squash-and-stretch, no fur strands. Full paws, ears and tails visible; no creature overlaps another panel. Perspective consistency, large legible figures. Main travel direction toward viewer-left.
Top row:
"Idle": grounded confident alert stance, four paws planted, slight head turn; chest remains rigid.
"Walk": clear quadruped step with near front paw reaching forward and opposite hind advancing, other feet support the weight; no airborne hopping.
"Run": dynamic low forward diagonal stride with a short suspension, front/rear legs articulating, level angular torso and stabilized segmented tail.
"Turn": planted inside paw, outside paws taking a wider arc, head leads body by 15 degrees, segmented tail counters the turn. Show a little more back/shoulder to explain the pivot.
Bottom row:
"Leap": hind-leg propulsion into an airborne arc, forelegs folding beneath rigid chest, tail straightening for balance; no torso stretching.
"Land": forepaws contacting first, elbow and knee joints bent to absorb weight, rear paws about to settle, chest shape unchanged.
"Depart": purposeful step/leap into a small clean pale-stone arch with a violet portal opening at the panel edge. Wolf looks forward, halfway approaching the portal; keep anatomy visible.
"Return": same wolf emerging from the matching portal, forepaws grounded, rear legs coming through, head lifted in a proud alert posture. This is a confident return, not a cute happy dance.
Reference specificity: do not make the wolf smaller/cuter, no round bloated body, no oversized eyes, no floppy paws. No gold crown or armor changes between poses. Keep it like a producible Roblox block-built guardian. No weapons, no scenery except the two small portal arches, no glowing fog, no watermark. This is a concept key-pose study, not a sprite sheet and not a claim that the game animations already exist.
