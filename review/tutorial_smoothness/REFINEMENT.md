# Current-game refinement

The seed shop retains keyed rows and buttons through wallet, stock and selection updates. This avoids destroying the current controller/touch target during a purchase. Only rows that leave the view are removed. Selection/purchase actions have 44px targets; locked crops have one unlock explanation. Growth times show hours, minutes and seconds, preserving visible watering improvements. Requests show their pending state on the same button, and late loading errors cannot overwrite a newer visit. The 34 market menu checks include 180 refreshes without new/destroyed controls, wallet styling recovery, delayed responses and short layouts.

Preview loading now has one owner in UIKit. Expedition portraits observe its readiness rather than issuing another preload for the same model. After 0.8 seconds a slow asset can no longer leave the available native geometry invisible. Late readiness does not replay its entrance. Replaced/destroyed portraits release their loading label and readiness listener. The shared worker skips preload for native-only geometry, stops mount waiting when removed, and avoids updating a viewport destroyed while loading. Failed optional assets can still use the native presentation.

The production callback tests pass 31 expedition presentation checks and seven shared viewport-readiness checks. These establish control lifecycle and fallback behavior with deterministic service mocks. They do not establish real-network asset timings, physical-device performance or native visual approval.
